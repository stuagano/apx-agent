Deploy to your workspace

Deploys the full stack — frontend, skills, and app — in ~5 minutes. No terminal required.

Your Databricks workspace URL.
  1. Open your workspace and click your profile icon (top right)
  2. Go to Settings → Developer tools → Access tokens
  3. Click Generate new token
  4. Give it a name (e.g. builder-app-deploy) and click Generate
  5. Copy the token and paste it above — it won't be shown again
Must be unique in your workspace. Stick with the default if unsure.
